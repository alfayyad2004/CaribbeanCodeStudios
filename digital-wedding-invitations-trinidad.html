Traditional Paper Invitations & Static PDFs

Why couples in Trinidad and Tobago are moving away from printed cards and basic Canva images:

  • Expensive Printing Costs: Printing 100–200 wedding invitations in Trinidad easily costs TT$2,500 to TT$6,000+.
  • Delivery Headaches: Spending weekends driving from Port of Spain to Chaguanas, San Fernando, and Arima just to hand-deliver envelopes.
  • Static PDFs Can't Take RSVPs: Sending a JPEG or PDF on WhatsApp still leaves you manually tallying who is coming in a notebook.
  • No Clickable Directions: Guests still message you on the wedding morning asking for the location pin.
  • Can't Update Details: If a ceremony time shifts by 30 minutes, printed cards are already permanent.
