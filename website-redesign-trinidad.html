Website Redesign Services in Trinidad

You already have a website. The problem is that it was built in 2018, takes eight seconds to load, looks broken on phones, and the person who built it has vanished. Sound familiar? You are not alone: most of the redesign inquiries we get in Trinidad start with some version of that exact story.

A redesign with CaribbeanCodeStudios is not a coat of paint. We rebuild your site on modern, fast, secure technology, keep your domain and your Google presence intact, and hand you full ownership of the result.

Signs Your Website Needs a Redesign

  • It fails the phone test. Open your site on a phone right now. If you have to pinch and zoom, you are losing the majority of your visitors, because most T&T traffic is mobile.
  • It loads slowly. More than three seconds on mobile data and visitors leave before seeing anything.
  • You cannot update it. The original developer is unreachable, the passwords are lost, or every small change costs money and takes weeks.
  • It looks dated. Customers judge your business by your website. A 2018 design tells them your business peaked in 2018.
  • It is invisible on Google. If searching your own services in your own town does not surface your site, it is not doing its job.
  • It runs on old WordPress. Outdated plugins are the number one way local business sites get hacked and blacklisted.

What a Typical "Redesign" Gets You Elsewhere

  • A new theme installed over the same slow platform
  • The same plugins waiting to break again
  • Rankings lost because nobody set up redirects
  • Continued dependence on whoever holds the passwords
  • Monthly fees that never end

What Our Redesign Gets You

  • A ground-up rebuild on fast, secure static technology
  • Your domain unchanged, email untouched
  • Rankings protected with proper 301 redirects and preserved SEO
  • Full ownership of the new site, no lock-in
  • One-time cost from TT$2,500, optional care plans only if you want them

How the Redesign Process Works

  1. Audit: We review your current site, note what ranks on Google and what customers actually use, and identify what to keep, improve, or drop.
  2. Rebuild: We design and build the new site on modern static technology, reusing your best content and adding what was missing.
  3. Migrate: Old page addresses are 301-redirected to their new homes so bookmarks, shared links, and Google rankings carry over.
  4. Launch: The new site goes live on your existing domain, usually within 3 to 7 business days of starting.
